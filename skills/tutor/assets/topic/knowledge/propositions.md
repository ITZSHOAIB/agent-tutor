# Propositions

Facts taught in this topic, one per row: a map of what was taught. The evidence of what the learner actually got right lives in the session logs, and every reading here cites it.

**Reading** is the latest status with its date, never a final verdict: `held` (checked right and confident) · `fragile` (right but unsure, or needed repair) · `taught` (no check yet).

**Evidence** lists question IDs from `sessions/` (e.g. `1.1-Q3, P1-Q7`), newest last.
