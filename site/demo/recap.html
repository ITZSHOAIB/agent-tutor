1.1 Why "espero que estés", not "estás"

After a wish or a request about someone else, the next verb changes form, because what follows isn't stated as fact.

Break it

Swapped "espero que" for "sé que" in my own email opener and predicted the verb would stay subjunctive. It flipped back to "estás": knowing states a fact, so the change disappears.

Your rule for your own work

When I write a wish or a request about a colleague (espero que, te pido que), the next verb changes. When I state what I know (sé que, creo que), it doesn't.
